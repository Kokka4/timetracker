Needs a label

Label one of these and every matching block, past and future, follows. The “around it” column is whose work was in front just before and just after.

Setup

The vocabulary this tracker describes your work in. All of it is yours: rename, recolour, delete, or start from nothing. Renaming re-labels your whole history the moment you do it.

Categories

What kind of work a block is. The tag says where each came from and how many rules use it.

Clients

Whose work it is. Add them here, or create one as you go from the review queue on the Dashboard. Archive the ones you have finished with to keep the picker short.

Starter packs

Optional bundles of rules for a kind of work. Turning one on only adds; turning it off takes back exactly what it added, and never a category you have started using yourself.

Rules

Every label you apply writes one of these, and every rule applies to your whole history, not just from now on. That is the learning: visit a site you have labelled before and it is attributed before you look. A site you have never labelled waits for you in the review queue.
